The project

Clinic operations, organized.

The system aims to bring routine clinic workflows into a single desktop environment, from patient registration and visit documentation to daily reporting and AI-assisted review.

♙

Patient records

Store patient details, medical history, allergies, previous visits, imaging references, and treatment plans in an organized record.

◷

Daily workflow

Manage the waiting queue, record examinations, track daily visits, and prepare financial summaries for the clinic.

✧

AI-assisted review

Send case details to a configurable AI provider and display a structured response, with explicit limits on clinical claims.
