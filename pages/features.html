Overview / Features
Product capabilities

Designed around the clinic's daily workflow.

Feature descriptions reflect the project README and application modules. Gallery screenshots use sample interface data for demonstration.

♙

Authentication & roles

Login with Argon2-based password hashing, session handling, and three documented roles: admin, doctor, and secretary. Sensitive pages are hidden from unauthorized roles.

AdminDoctorSecretary
▦

Dashboard

Dynamic welcome, shortcuts, six statistic cards, patient queue actions, a beep on calling a patient, and charts for seven-day income and new patients.

♧

Patient management

Add, edit, delete, and search patient records by name, code, or phone. Open patient details and the associated folder from the patient table.

▤

Patient medical profile

Review medical history, allergies, chronic conditions, previous surgeries, current medications, family history, latest complaint, vital signs, diagnosis, and treatment plan.

✚

Visits & examinations

Record complaints, pain location, clinical examination, ROM and tests, ICD-10 code, examination fee, treatment and rehabilitation plan, and up to three prescribed medications.

♧

Patient queue

Add patients to the queue, call the next patient, mark the current examination complete, and track waiting, called, and completed states.

♙

Employee management

Manage employee contact details, job, salary, payday, hire date, and notes. Browse employee cards and search by name or role.

−

Expense tracking

Record expenses in eight categories, calculate daily and monthly totals, filter by category, and search by expense name.

▥

Daily reports

Choose a date and review patient counts, new patients, visits, gross income, expenses, and net income, with a print option.

⚙

Settings & services

Configure the default examination price and maintain clinic services, categories, and prices.

⇄

Bilingual interface

Switch between Arabic and English, with automatic RTL/LTR direction changes as documented in the README.

✧

AI assistant

Submit case information for structured analysis in nine sections, then save the result as PDF or print it.

⚒

Surgeries & payments

Record orthopedic surgeries, schedule dates, link operations to patient records, and track surgery-related payments.

▣

Clinic stock

Maintain stock items, record incoming and outgoing movements, review stock value, and identify items below their reorder threshold.

◉

Clinic reminders

Collect reminders for upcoming surgeries and follow-ups, as well as salary due dates, to support day-to-day planning.

♧

Payroll tracking

Track employee salary schedules, payments, and monthly amounts due alongside employee records.

Typical workflow

Register or find a patient

Use the patient list to create a record or search for an existing one.

Record the visit

Select the patient, document the complaint and examination, and save the visit.

Manage the queue

Call the next waiting patient and mark the visit complete after the examination.

Review operations

Use reports and expense summaries to review the clinic's daily activity.

Clinical note

The application is a management tool. Any AI-generated clinical analysis must be reviewed by a qualified clinician and should not be treated as a definitive diagnosis.
